## RoyaleAPI cr-api-data / cr-api-assets

Card statistics (`flybrain/envs/royale/data/cards.json`) are built from
RoyaleAPI's cr-api-data. The real-game bot downloads the official card
pictures from RoyaleAPI's cr-api-assets at run time (not stored here).
